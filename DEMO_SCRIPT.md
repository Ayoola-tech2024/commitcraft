## 🎙️ 2-Minute Action Script

### ⏱️ [0:00 – 0:15] Intro
*(Screen open to `https://commitcraft.pages.dev` in full screen)*
> **Read aloud:**  
> "Hey judges! My name is **Ayoola Damisile**, and this is **CommitCraft** — a visual Git sandbox, error rescue matrix, and guided quest platform built specifically for beginner developers."

---

### ⏱️ [0:15 – 0:45] Visual Git Sandbox
*(Click **`+ commit`** $\to$ click **`+ branch (ui)`** $\to$ click bubble **`c2`** to show details)*
> **Read aloud:**  
> "In a standard terminal, Git feels like an invisible black box. 
> 
> With CommitCraft, every single command is drawn on screen in real time with animated branch lines and glowing commit nodes. You can make commits, create branches, and click any bubble to inspect its details and build a real mental model of Git."

---

### ⏱️ [0:45 – 1:15] 16-Error Decoder
*(Click **Error Decoder** tab $\to$ click **`Unrelated histories`** chip $\to$ click **`Copy Command`** $\to$ click **`Try in Sandbox`**)*
> **Read aloud:**  
> "Next is our **Error Decoder** — your 2 AM rescue kit. 
> 
> When you run into nightmare errors like *'refusing to merge unrelated histories'* or port collisions, it instantly translates the error into plain English, explains why it broke, gives you a 1-click copy fix, and lets you test it right in the visual sandbox."

---

### ⏱️ [1:15 – 1:45] Guided Missions & Confetti
*(Click **Guided Missions** tab $\to$ click **`Run Suggested Command`** 3 times $\to$ **watch confetti explode!**)*
> **Read aloud:**  
> "To build real confidence, we created **Guided Missions**. 
> 
> It walks you through making your first commit step-by-step with live terminal validation. When you complete the quest, you get celebratory confetti and unlock trophies saved directly in your browser!"

---

### ⏱️ [1:45 – 2:05] Conflict Lab & Outro
*(Click **Merge Conflict Lab** tab $\to$ click **`Accept Both Changes`** $\to$ click **`Commit Resolution & Merge`**)*
> **Read aloud:**  
> "Finally, our **Conflict Lab** takes the fear out of merge conflicts by showing both versions side-by-side with 1-click resolution buttons.
> 
> CommitCraft is live right now at **commitcraft.pages.dev** and open-source on GitHub. Thank you to Harshil, Sudheer, and FirstCommit for this amazing opportunity!"
