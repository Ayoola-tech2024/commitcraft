## 🎙️ Word-for-Word Teleprompter Script

### ⏱️ [0:00 – 0:35] Introduction & The Real Problem
*(Have `https://commitcraft.pages.dev` open in full screen on the **Visual Git Sandbox** tab)*

> **What to do on screen:**  
> Start with the browser showing the top of CommitCraft with the glowing nodes and header stats.
>
> **Read aloud:**  
> "Hello FirstCommit hackathon organizers, judges Harshil and Sudheer, and fellow developers! My name is **Ayoola Damisile**, and today I am thrilled to present **CommitCraft** — the interactive visual Git sandbox, 16-error emergency rescue matrix, and guided quest platform designed specifically for beginner developers.
>
> When beginners first learn to code, they don’t quit because of logic or `if/else` statements. They quit when their terminal throws a terrifying red wall of text — like `fatal: refusing to merge unrelated histories` or `EADDRINUSE: 3000`. In a standard terminal, Git feels like an invisible black box.
>
> We built CommitCraft to make Git and coding completely visual, tactile, and stress-free."

---

### ⏱️ [0:35 – 1:20] Demo 1: The Animated Visual Git Sandbox
*(Stay on the **Visual Git Sandbox** tab)*

> **What to do on screen:**  
> 1. Click the **`+ commit`** quick pill button. Watch the new node `c3` animate smoothly onto the canvas with glowing links.  
> 2. Click the **`+ branch (ui)`** quick pill button. Notice the branch `feature/ui` split off in emerald green.  
> 3. Click **`+ commit`** again to add a commit on the new branch.  
> 4. Click **`checkout main`** to see the active `HEAD` pointer jump back to main.  
> 5. Click on the commit circle **`c2`** to open the **Commit Details Inspector Modal**, showing the commit SHA, author, and parent hashes. Close the modal.
>
> **Read aloud:**  
> "First, let's explore our **Visual Git Sandbox**.
> 
> Instead of memorizing commands in the dark, every Git action is rendered live on an animated SVG canvas powered by Framer Motion. 
> 
> When I click `+ commit`, a new commit node smoothly links to the parent commit using dynamic cubic bezier curves. When I branch out into `feature/ui`, an isolated branch lane is created. When I switch back to `main`, the glowing `HEAD` pointer shifts in real-time.
> 
> Beginners can also click on any commit node to inspect its exact metadata, author, and parent hashes, building a concrete mental model of how Git actually works under the hood."

---

### ⏱️ [1:20 – 2:05] Demo 2: 16-Error Terminal Emergency Rescue Matrix
*(Click on the **Error Decoder** tab in the top navigation bar)*

> **What to do on screen:**  
> 1. In the search box, click the chip **`Unrelated histories`**.  
> 2. Scroll down to show the 3-part breakdown: **1. What Happened**, **2. Why It Happened**, and **3. How to Fix It**.  
> 3. Click the **`Copy Command`** button and show the green *Copied!* feedback checkmark.  
> 4. Click the **`Try in Sandbox`** button at the bottom of the card to show how it seamlessly teleports you back to the sandbox with the fix command executed!
>
> **Read aloud:**  
> "Next is our **Error Decoder** — the 2 AM emergency rescue kit for beginners.
> 
> We curated the 16 most common beginner nightmare errors across Git, NPM, Node.js, JavaScript, and React. 
> 
> When a beginner pastes a scary error or searches for `unrelated histories`, CommitCraft instantly diagnoses it with three clear sections:
> First, **What happened in plain English** without confusing jargon.
> Second, **Why it happened**, teaching the underlying concept.
> And third, **The exact 1-click copyable fix**.
> 
> Even better, for Git errors, clicking `Try in Sandbox` automatically switches tabs and executes the fix right inside the visual canvas!"

---

### ⏱️ [2:05 – 2:45] Demo 3: Guided Beginner Missions & Confetti Rewards
*(Click on the **Guided Missions** tab in the top navigation bar)*

> **What to do on screen:**  
> 1. Show the **Achievement Trophy Showcase** at the top.  
> 2. On Mission 1 (*The Genesis Commit*), show Step 1 is active.  
> 3. Click the blue **`Run Suggested Command`** button (or type `git init` into the quest terminal).  
> 4. Notice the step check off with a green checkmark!  
> 5. Click **`Run Suggested Command`** for Step 2 (`git add .`) and Step 3 (`git commit -m "feat: my first commit"`).  
> 6. Watch the full-screen **confetti fireworks explosion** and the **Genesis Committer Badge Modal** appear!
>
> **Read aloud:**  
> "To build real muscle memory, CommitCraft includes **Gamified Guided Missions**.
> 
> In Mission 1, *The Genesis Commit*, our live regex validator guides developers through initializing a repository, staging files, and recording their first commit. 
> 
> As each step completes, the validator provides instant real-time feedback. And when the mission is accomplished, full-screen celebratory confetti bursts across the screen, unlocking an achievement trophy that persists in the browser's local storage!"

---

### ⏱️ [2:45 – 3:15] Demo 4: Merge Conflict Lab & Closing
*(Click on the **Merge Conflict Lab** tab in the top navigation bar)*

> **What to do on screen:**  
> 1. Show the side-by-side comparison: **Current Change (HEAD: main)** on the left vs **Incoming Change** on the right.  
> 2. Click **`Accept Both Changes (Combined)`**. Show the clean preview below.  
> 3. Click **`Commit Resolution & Merge`** to trigger the victory confetti and XP points (+75 XP).  
> 4. Conclude the video with enthusiasm.
>
> **Read aloud:**  
> "Finally, our **Merge Conflict Lab** demystifies the single most intimidating part of Git. 
> 
> Instead of scary conflict markers, it displays a side-by-side 3-way diff. Beginners can choose to accept their changes, their teammate's changes, or combine both with one click.
> 
> CommitCraft is 100% client-side, offline-ready, deployed live on Cloudflare Pages at **commitcraft.pages.dev**, and has a clean 13-commit history on GitHub.
> 
> Thank you Harshil, Sudheer, and FirstCommit for organizing this amazing event!"
